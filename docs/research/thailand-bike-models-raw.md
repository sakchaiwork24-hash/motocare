# Thailand Popular Motorcycle Models and Service Intervals Research

## Popular Brands and Models
The motorcycle market in Thailand is heavily dominated by Japanese brands, primarily Honda and Yamaha. 

### Honda
- **Honda Wave:** The undisputed king of Thai roads, famous for durability, fuel efficiency, and cheap maintenance.
- **Scooters (Scoopy, Click, PCX160, Giorno+):** Highly popular for urban commuting.
- **Adventure/Performance (ADV 160/350, Forza 350, CRF300L):** Popular for those wanting more power or touring capability.

### Yamaha
- **Scooters (Grand Filano Hybrid, NMAX, XMAX):** Strong competitors with stylish designs and premium features.
- **Commuters (Finn, PG-1):** Popular for economy and lifestyle/outdoor use.

### Other Brands
- **GPX:** A Thai brand gaining popularity for stylish and affordable bikes.
- Premium brands like Triumph, Harley-Davidson, and Ducati are popular in the big bike segment.

## Rough Service Intervals and Maintenance (General Guidelines)

Most commuter bikes and scooters in Thailand follow a similar, mileage-based maintenance schedule.

### Standard Intervals
- **Initial Break-in Service:** Typically at 1,000 km (crucial for oil change and initial checks).
- **Regular Service:** Every 4,000 km to 6,000 km depending on the specific model and usage.

### Key Maintenance Items
- **Engine Oil:** Replaced at regular intervals (every 4,000 - 6,000 km). Highly recommended to change more frequently if used heavily in dusty or hot conditions.
- **Drive Chain:** Needs inspection, cleaning, and lubrication roughly every 500 km.
- **Air Filter:** Inspected regularly; replaced more often in Thailand's dusty environments.
- **Spark Plug:** Often replaced every 8,000 km or per manual specs.
- **Brakes & Fluid:** Fluid replacement roughly every 2 years; pads checked at every service.

*Note: Always consult the specific model's owner's manual for exact requirements.*
